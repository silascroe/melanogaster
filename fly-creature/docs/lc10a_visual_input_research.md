# LC10a visual input: evidence boundary for the pursuit model

This note asks what visual input can be assigned to the **individual LC10a body IDs in MaleCNS v1.0**. It is a source review, not a receptive-field reconstruction or a validation of the playable model. The [MaleCNS release](https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/) provides neuron annotations, connectivity, synapse locations, and skeletons; the [official LC10a Cell Type Explorer page](https://reiserlab.github.io/celltype-explorer-drosophila-male-cns/types/LC10a_R.html) lists 275 LC10a cells (140 right, 135 left). Neither a synapse count nor an LC10a type label is a measured visual response for a particular body ID.

## What experiments support

LC10 visual projection neurons respond preferentially to small moving objects through a motion based center-surround mechanism, and suppressing LC10 disrupts directed male courtship. These are population and genetic intervention results; they do not specify an input waveform for each MaleCNS LC10a cell. [Ribeiro et al., Cell 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30033367/).

LC10a activity is spatially structured. Imaging work found local receptive fields that tile visual space, and LC10a responded differently to opposite directions of a moving figure edge. The figure-ground experiment also found little direction discrimination for ground motion, illustrating why a generic “moving object” flag loses relevant stimulus structure. [Aptekar et al., Journal of Neuroscience 2015, Figs. 4–5](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25972183/). Anatomical work found that LC10 axon terminals retain some retinotopic organization in the anterior optic tubercle, unlike most LC glomeruli. That supports a spatial hypothesis, not an exact visual angle for one MaleCNS body ID. [Wu et al., eLife 2016](https://elifesciences.org/articles/21022).

In male courtship experiments, LC10a responses to moving targets increase with P1 associated sexual arousal. Responses were stronger for progressive than regressive motion, and P1 activation amplified responses to several stimulus classes rather than replacing the receptive field with a new one. Thus fixed, equal current into all LC10a cells on a side omits both motion direction and internal state. The imaging regions of interest were functional signals, not a crosswalk to MaleCNS EM body IDs. [Hindmarsh Sten et al., Nature 2021](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-03714-w).

## The AOTU019 / AOTU025 / DNa02 route

[Collie et al., Neuron 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12212441/) found that AOTU019 preferentially samples LC10a cells associated with frontal visual space, while AOTU025 preferentially samples more lateral LC10a cells. Recordings from **male flies** confirmed frontal bias in AOTU019 responses and more lateral responses in AOTU025. Their AOTU receptive field *estimates* mapped LC10a lobula arbor position onto a retinal axis; they were not direct measurements of every presynaptic LC10a cell's visual field. The two response zones overlap. [Collie et al., Fig. 1 and Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12212441/).

The same study found stronger ipsiversive motion direction selectivity in AOTU019 than AOTU025, and AOTU019 gain increased with fast running and arousal. Suppressing AOTU019 in aroused males impaired pursuit steering, but did not abolish it; the authors also identified smaller parallel AOTU paths. A fixed position-only input and one fixed output gain therefore omit tested features of the real circuit. [Collie et al., Figs. 3–6](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12212441/).

Collie's **connectome** analysis used **FAFB/FlyWire, a female adult brain**, even though its physiology and pursuit behavior used males. It excluded connections below five synapses and estimated retinal positions from that female reconstruction. The MaleCNS v1.0 edges in this project are separately measured anatomy; corresponding type names do not prove identical per-cell contacts or receptive fields across animals or datasets. [Collie et al., Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12212441/); [MaleCNS release](https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/).

DNa02 bilateral firing differences correlate approximately linearly with turning velocity in walking flies, and DNa02 is associated especially with rapid, high gain steering. Its activity also reflects walking state and converging nonvisual pathways. This supports using DNa02 as a *candidate steering readout*, but does not calibrate the creature's subtraction of model **voltage**, its conversion to radians per second, or its leg motion. [Rayshubskiy et al., eLife 2025](https://elifesciences.org/articles/102230); [Collie et al., Fig. 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12212441/).

## What MaleCNS can tell us next

The [official LC10a Cell Type Explorer page](https://reiserlab.github.io/celltype-explorer-drosophila-male-cns/types/LC10a_R.html) exposes **population** lobula spatial-coverage plots, a neuron viewer, and per-neuron skeleton downloads. The [MaleCNS download page](https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/) exposes per-synapse coordinates and skeletons in MaleCNS EM space. These sources can support a new, explicitly derived anatomical estimate: locate each selected LC10a's lobula dendrites or input synapses, register the lobula axis to visual space, and compare candidate frontal/lateral subsets with their AOTU019/AOTU025 connection weights. The population plot alone cannot assign an azimuth or motion preference to an individual selected body ID. [MaleCNS Cell Type Explorer](https://reiserlab.github.io/celltype-explorer-drosophila-male-cns/types/LC10a_R.html); [MaleCNS release data descriptions](https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/); [Collie et al., receptive-field estimation method](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12212441/).

For the current 281-cell model, **whole-side activation is an engineered control input**. A frontal/lateral split based only on AOTU connection strength would be a *connectivity-derived hypothesis*, not a measured receptive-field label: the AOTU sampling zones overlap, and synapse count is not visual tuning. An anatomy-derived per-cell map would be a stronger input hypothesis, but would still require registration and physiology to claim retinal degrees or response amplitude. The existing intact-versus-bridge-removal comparison tests dependence on the selected modeled edges under the chosen stimulus; it cannot by itself establish that a real fly would pursue this target with these cell currents. [MaleCNS release data descriptions](https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/); [Collie et al., Fig. 1 and Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12212441/); [Hindmarsh Sten et al., LC10a physiology](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-03714-w).

Practical next comparison: keep model coupling, timing, decoder, and target paths fixed; compare the present whole-side input with a clearly labeled frontal/lateral **hypothesis**, and include the same bridge removal and a non-connectome wiring control. Record which body IDs receive each input and the evidence used to place them. Any improvement is a property of that modeled input and controller until a per-cell MaleCNS eyemap or matching physiological measurement supports the assignment. [Collie et al., Methods](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12212441/); [MaleCNS data access](https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/).

**Follow-up, 2026-09-26:** The [skeleton-derived input experiment](visual_input_experiment.md) now makes this comparison with explicit body IDs, wiring shuffles, bridge removal, and a direct-bearing controller. Its anatomy-based input aligned with the selected wiring but tracked worse than both the whole-side input and the direct rule on all five new paths. It remains an offline hypothesis.

**Further follow-up, 2026-09-26:** The official MaleCNS annotations do include
`assignedOlHex1/2` for many *upstream medulla-column neurons*, even though
the 275 LC10a rows do not have these columns filled. The
[Nern et al. optic-lobe analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12119369/)
defines the corresponding eye-map coordinate system. We used incoming
column-to-LC10a connections and skeleton position to derive an
[independently oriented map](visual_motion_experiment.md) without consulting
the AOTU output weights. This establishes a stronger anatomical ordering,
not measured single-cell receptive fields or an exact visual-angle scale.
